## Sabre Browser — Core Features (User-Facing)

---

### 1. 📡 Never-Die Browsing
- **Websites keep retrying** — if a page fails to load, Sabre keeps trying in the background until it works
- **No more "This page isn't working"** — the browser just keeps at it
- **Network-agnostic** — works just as well on spotty 3G as on fiber

---

### 2. 💾 Form Recovery (Auto-Save)
- **Anything you type** into any form on any website is saved locally
- If your internet dies mid-submission, your work is **not lost**
- Syncs automatically when connection returns
- No more retyping long applications, reviews, or comments

---

### 3. 📚 Fossil Cache (Offline Reading)
- **Pages you visit are stored locally** — full copies
- Access them **even when completely offline**
- Like having a personal offline archive of the web
- Perfect for documentation, articles, reference material

---

### 4. ⬇️ Supercharged Downloads
- **Large files download faster** — split into chunks, downloaded in parallel
- **Downloads never fail** — if a chunk drops, only that chunk retries
- Perfect for 1GB+ files, ISOs, video projects
- Resumes automatically if connection drops

---

### 5. 🚫 Built-in Adblocking (No Extensions Needed)
- Blocks ads, trackers, and malware **out of the box**
- No extension to install, no performance hit
- Works on every site, every time
- Faster page loads, less data usage

---

### 6. 🔐 Actually-Private Incognito
- **No trace left behind** — not even on your SSD
- All private session data lives in memory only
- When you close the window, it's **permanently wiped**
- Not "private browsing" — **actually private**

---

### 7. 🔗 Locked Bookmarks (Link Mask)
- Save sensitive URLs behind a custom name
- Protected by a 6-digit PIN
- Shared devices? No problem — your bookmarks stay hidden
- Quick access without typing long URLs

---

### 8. 🔍 Private Search (No AI, No Tracking)
- Default search engine is privacy-first (Brave Search or self-hosted)
- No AI summaries shoved in your face
- No personalized tracking, no telemetry
- Just clean, unfiltered search results


---

## What Sabre *Is*

A browser for people who are:
- **Tired of losing work** to bad connections
- **Sick of ads** and trackers
- **Privacy-conscious** but not paranoid
- **Offline often** or on unreliable networks
- **Nothing ecosystem fans** (eventually)
- **Developers** who want clean dev tools

---

## What Sabre *Isn't*

- A Chrome replacement for everyone
- A feature-bloated Swiss Army knife
- A corporate product with telemetry
- An AI-powered browser
- A cloud-first browser

---

## In One Sentence

> Sabre is a **resilient, privacy-first browser** that never gives up, never loses your data, and never tracks you — built for the real, unreliable web.
